02 / THREE LENSES

Separate the signal from the story.

A change in word choice need not be a change in conviction. A stated goal need not be an action. A later memory need not be a contemporary forecast.

measured

How was it recorded?

Dates, frequency, length and structure. Measurements of a journal's form, with missingness and sampling made visible.

provisional

How was it understood?

Possible changes in explanations, priorities and contradictions. Interpretations require contextual reading and alternatives.

unanswered

What actually happened?

Predictions and life outcomes cannot be scored when later evidence has not been adjudicated.

04 / METHODS & LIMITS

Uncertainty belongs in the exhibit.

This is a research process, not a psychological diagnosis, a growth score, or an account of all lived experiences.

What the archive records is selective. Different periods may differ in writing frequency, genre, memory, available technology and willingness to describe particular experiences. Statistical repeatability and interpretive truth are separate questions.

  • MEASURED Counts and descriptive properties of text
  • PROVISIONAL Historical interpretations awaiting contextual verification
  • NOT ASSESSED Forecast outcomes without completed review
  • NOT ESTABLISHED Causal explanation or universal growth claim
